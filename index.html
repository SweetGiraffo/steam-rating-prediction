Phase 2

Data Cleaning & Exploratory Analysis

Profiling the rating landscape: discovering extreme positive skew (88.8% vs 11.2%), the paid-vs-free rating gap, and key genre/tag correlations.

"Indie" (94.0%) and "Story Rich" (93.4%) strongly push sentiment upwards, whereas "Multiplayer" drops to 83.2%.
Genre Proportions & Rating Rates
Indie and Adventure lead in rating percentage, while competitive MMO and pure Action show greater review dispersion.
Phase 3

Feature Engineering & Selection

Transforming raw multilabel text and raw counters into 474 features, pruning 192 low-variance tags, and curating the top 60 predictors with zero data leakage.

Derived Domain Metrics

  • price_per_hour_playtime: Value proposition metric: price / (playtime_hours + 1).
  • engagement_rate: Ratio of total review volume to estimated owner midpoint.
  • discount_percentage: Normalized promotional discount proportion.
  • ccu_to_owners_ratio: Concurrent active player ratio relative to total installs.
  • MultiLabel Encoding: One-hot matrices across 434 tags and 24 genres.
  • VarianceThreshold (0.01): Pruned tags used in <1% of games (<25 titles) to prevent sparse matrix degradation.
Preliminary Top Feature Importances
Initial Random Forest Gini ranking identifying log_ccu, price, tag_Multiplayer, and genre_Indie as primary split candidates.
