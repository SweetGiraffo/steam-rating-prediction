Phase 3

Feature Engineering & Selection

Binarization across 434 community tags and 24 genres, engineering domain value ratios, and variance filtering with zero target leakage.

Engineered Feature Components

  • price_per_hour_playtime: Value metric computed as price / (playtime_hours + 1).
  • engagement_rate: Ratio of total review volume to estimated owner midpoint.
  • discount_percentage: Normalized discount proportion.
  • ccu_to_owners_ratio: Concurrent active player ratio relative to total installs.
  • MultiLabel Encoding: One-hot matrices across 434 tags and 24 genres.
  • VarianceThreshold (0.01): Pruned 192 low-variance tags active in <1% of games.
  • Data Leakage Prevention: Excluded raw review counts and review ratios from training features.
Preliminary Random Forest Gini Importances
log_ccu, initial_price, tag_Multiplayer, and genre_Indie ranked as top predictors.
